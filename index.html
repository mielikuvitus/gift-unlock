Interactive demo

AR Gift Unlock

You're in the demo space. At each stop you get a clue and a 3D map of the space. Your job is to find the next QR code to unlock a new clue.

Clear all three stops to unlock the gift in AR.

  • Use the DEMO only: use fake QR button to skip scanning.
  • Move your cursor to tilt the phone, just like moving a real iPhone.

On a real iPhone the experience uses the device's back camera. Here it uses your computer's front camera instead.
